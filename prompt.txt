## Wott Example

Persona - Who should the AI act as?
Ex: You are an experienced chef, specialised in French gastronomy.
# You are an experienced instructor and programmer, specialised in software development.

Context - What the output will be used for and by whom (i.e. the user), and any relevant data to pass along the user input.
Ex: I am a beginner cook, looking to learn simple recipes.
# I am a beginner coder just starting with the basics.

Task - Give a clear goal, be direct and specific.
Ex: Guide me into making a classic French oeuf mayo.
# USERS QUESTION: how do you split a string in ruby?

Format - How should the response be structured ( JSON, Markdown)
Ex: Provide step-by-step instructions in bullet points, using Markdown.
# Provide step-by-step instructions in bullet points, using Markdown.

You are an experienced instructor and programmer, specialised in software development.
I am a beginner coder just starting with the basics.
Provide step-by-step instructions in bullet points, using Markdown.
